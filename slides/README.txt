PUT YOUR SLIDES PDF HERE
========================

1. In Google Slides: File > Download > PDF document.
2. Upload the PDF into this folder (for example slides.pdf).
3. In config.js set:

     slidesPdf: "slides/slides.pdf",

   (The lib folder, one level up, must be uploaded too: it is the PDF reader.)

Each page of the PDF becomes one slide, moved from the presenter window.
Animations and embedded videos in Google Slides do not carry over.
