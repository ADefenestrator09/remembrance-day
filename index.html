Troubleshooting

• Check the device volume and that the tab isn't muted (look for a speaker icon on the tab).
• Make sure the correct speakers or projector audio is selected in your system sound settings.
• Bluetooth speakers sometimes need a few seconds to wake up. Try the tone again.
• On a laptop connected by HDMI, set the output device to the display/projector.
